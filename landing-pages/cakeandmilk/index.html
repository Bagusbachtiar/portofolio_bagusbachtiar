Our Story

A Corner of
Warmth & Wonder

"Every cake tells a story.
Every cup holds a memory."

Cake & Milk was born from a simple dream: to create a space where every visit feels like coming home. Our pastry chef trained in Lyon and brings classical French techniques together with seasonal local produce.

We roast our own single-origin beans and pull every shot with intention. From our signature Burnt Basque to our miso caramel choux, each creation is made fresh before sunrise.

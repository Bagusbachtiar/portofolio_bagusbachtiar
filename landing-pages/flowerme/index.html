Our Story

Rooted in Nature,
Grown With Love

Founded in 2008 by botanist-turned-florist Clara Whitmore, Petal & Stem began as a small studio with one belief: flowers have the power to speak what words cannot.

Today, we source the finest seasonal blooms from local growers and international farms, crafting arrangements that balance artistry with nature's own beauty.

Our Story

Born from
the Sea

Founded in 2012 by Chef Marco Delacroix, myseafood began as a small harbor-side bistro with one mission: serve the ocean's best, honestly.

Today we partner with 14 local fishing families who deliver their catch before dawn. Every dish tells a story — of the waters, the fisherman, and the hands that prepared it.
